# Budget static hosting — phase one

**Status:** Agreed service scope, pending website publication and written Terms of Service. This is planning documentation, not a signed contract. Owner: Kyle Givler.

## Audience and pricing
- $149/year for **basic managed static website hosting**.
- Customers can bring an existing compatible site, subject to a review; a website build is not required.
- For personal sites, nonprofits, portfolios, and simple small-business brochure sites. Not for business-critical sites requiring contractual availability.
- Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, images and other static assets only. No client-run server processes, databases, WordPress, PHP, custom APIs or e-commerce.
- This is a separate hosting offer from the $299/$499 introductory website build packages.

## Included
- Hosting on my managed shared infrastructure, deployment management and HTTPS.
- Basic uptime/technical monitoring and reasonable maintenance.
- Where offered and configured, the **operator-run contact form infrastructure** (not general-purpose backend hosting).
- **One minor content update annually**: change contact details or hours, edit a small amount of existing text, or replace an existing image. No new pages, redesigns, significant copy work, new functionality or integrations.
- **$50/hour, 30-minute minimum applies only to additional content updates for static websites on this basic managed hosting plan.** New pages, custom development, features, integrations, and all other services are separately quoted. Do not advertise $50/hour as a general website-development rate.

## Availability and support
- Best effort. **No SLA, guaranteed uptime, guaranteed response/recovery time, uptime credits, or 24/7/emergency support.**
- Normally respond within a few business days, **not guaranteed**. Contact is initially via the dev.kgivler.com form or LinkedIn. Tickets via HappyPortal are a future possibility, not included now.
- I use the **same infrastructure for my own services and dev.kgivler.com**; this is a practical incentive to maintain it, **not an uptime promise**. Re-check the accuracy of this statement if hosting topology changes.

## Backups and ownership
- **No customer backup service, backup guarantee, or guaranteed restore included.** Customers maintain independent copies of their site files and content. Any internal system backups are not a service entitlement.
- Customer owns their domain and customer-specific website files/content subject to the project agreement. Domain registration and renewal are the customer's responsibility.
- Sites are portable. Help selecting/migrating to another provider is available by separately agreed paid work; not guaranteed as part of hosting.
- Possible future: optional self-service ZIP download via HappyPortal if HappyPages architecture permits. **Do not advertise this as current functionality.**

## Payment and expiration
- Annual payment; **no refunds** for voluntary cancellation, except where required by law or separately agreed.
- **14-day renewal grace period** after the paid term ends. Renewal remains possible during that window; service may be suspended during that period. After 14 days, files and service may be removed. No guarantee of preservation or recovery after contract end.
- Avoid promising the site will remain online for all 14 days; clarify suspension and reminders in written terms before sales.
- Details of renewal notices, payments and acceptance of terms still need to be finalized.

## Other offerings and boundaries
- No paid business-critical/premium managed-hosting tier in phase one.
- Can provide separately billed help choosing and configuring a more suitable commercial host for higher-availability requirements, **without promising to personally supply an SLA**.
- Resource/fair-use and prohibited-use rules, privacy disclosures, and a plain-language Hosting TOS are **must-do before taking paid hosting customers**. Do not invent numerical quotas or retention guarantees until defined.
- A dedicated `/hosting/` information page should explain the offer in customer-facing language; a homepage service card, the `/websites/` package page and hosting FAQ should link to it.
