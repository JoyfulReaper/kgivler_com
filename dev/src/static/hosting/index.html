Good fit

Who this is for

Personal sites, portfolios, nonprofit pages, and small-business sites built from HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, and other static files. I can review an existing site before taking it on.

It doesn't include WordPress, databases, your own server-side code, e-commerce, booking software, or always-on applications. New pages, redesigns, integrations, and substantial content changes are separate development work.

For larger changes, my published website update rate is $50/hour with a 30-minute minimum where applicable, or I'll quote the work separately.

Availability

I use this hosting too.

I run my own websites and services on the same infrastructure, including dev.kgivler.com, the site you're reading. When it has problems, my own services are affected too.

I have every reason to keep the servers running well. But this is still best-effort hosting. There's no uptime guarantee, Service Level Agreement (SLA), guaranteed repair time, or compensation for downtime. Maintenance and outages can happen.

There is no 24/7 or emergency support. I normally respond to messages within a few business days, but that isn't guaranteed. For now, use the contact form or LinkedIn.

Backups & ownership

Your files and domain stay yours.

Backups and guaranteed recovery are not included. Keep a separate copy of your site files and important content. I may keep internal infrastructure backups, but those aren't a customer backup or restore service.

You own your domain and are responsible for its registration and renewal. Your website files remain yours under the project agreement, and you can move to a different host. If you want help moving or configuring it, we can agree on a separate fee.

Billing & expiration

Annual billing, with 14 days to renew.

Hosting costs $149 per year. Fees are nonrefundable if you cancel early, except where required by law or separately agreed.

After your paid term expires, there's a 14-day renewal grace period. The site may be suspended during that period. Afterward, hosting and files may be removed. No data retention or recovery is guaranteed after the agreement ends.

Please keep an independent copy of your website rather than relying on the renewal window as a backup.

Other options

Need more than budget static hosting?

If your site is business-critical or needs contractual uptime guarantees, this probably isn't the right hosting plan. I can help you choose and set up a more suitable third-party provider for a separately agreed fee.

You can also choose another host from the start. You don't have to use mine.
