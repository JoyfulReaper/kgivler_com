Optional Managed Hosting

$149/year

If you'd like me to handle hosting, this includes:

  • HTTPS/SSL
  • Hosting and deployment management
  • Basic uptime/technical monitoring
  • Keeping the contact form infrastructure functioning
  • One minor content update per year

A minor content update means changing contact details or business hours, editing a small amount of existing text, or replacing an existing image. New pages, layout changes, and new features aren't minor updates.

This is best-effort hosting for static sites only: no uptime SLA, included backups, or 24/7 support. You're responsible for keeping a copy of your site files. Annual fees are nonrefundable except as required by law; there's a 14-day renewal grace period after expiry, but no guaranteed data retention after the agreement ends.
